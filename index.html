About the Venerable

Venerable Freeborn Avwerosuoghene Onokpite (JP)

A seasoned Anglican priest, theologian, and public servant based in Warri, Delta State. With a calling rooted in deliverance, restoration, and servant-leadership, he combines spiritual ministry with active community service.

Ecclesiastical Career

Served in key parishes including All Saints' Anglican Chapel (DSC) and Christ's Anglican Church (Okwagbe Waterside). Collated as Venerable in March 2022.

Public Service

Appointed in December 2023 as State Deputy Coordinator (Central) on Religious Matters, Delta State.

Scholarship

A researcher in African Christianity who has documented charismatic movements in the Niger Delta.

Research & Writings

Emergence of Charismatic Movement in Urhobo, Niger Delta, Nigeria (2022)

Saudi Journal of Humanities and Social Sciences

Anglican Fasting and Prayer Society and Development of Christianity in Urhobo, 1990-2017 (2022)
