MEET THE REBORN EZRA

A Divine Call to Restore Truth

Pastor E.D Tambou, PhD, is a respected theologian, seasoned lecturer, and devoted servant of God. He hails from Ayakoromo Town, Burutu L.G.A., Delta State, and is Izon by tribe. He holds a Doctor of Philosophy, PhD in World Religion because he studied Religious and Cultural Studies, from the University of Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria.

He currently serves as a Lecturer at The Redeemed Christian Theological College, Redemption City of God, Ogun State, Nigeria; where he mentors and trains emerging Christian leaders and ministers. His teaching ministry is marked by clarity, biblical depth, and prophetic insight.

Fondly called “Rabbi” by his students for his profound understanding of the Scriptures, he prefers to be called “The Reborn Ezra,” signifying his divine call to restore truth, purity, and revival through sound teaching and prayer.

Beyond theology, Dr. E.D Tambou is also a Certified Sports Psychologist, Football Coach, and Physical Fitness Coach, reflecting his belief in holistic development—nurturing both spiritual and physical well-being. He is married to Dcns. A. Nkeonyeasua with male and female children.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before You Reach Out

"If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally." — James 1:5

Are the books really free?

Yes. Every title under "Free Downloads" is free for personal study and classroom use. If you are blessed, a free-will offering to Zenith Bank 2007780413 (E.D. Tambou) helps print more and fund research. Premium titles live on Selar.

How do I get the student course materials?

Scroll to the Student Resource Center, search by course code (e.g. HEB 101), and tap Download. Files marked PLACEHOLDER are being finalized — message Dr. Tambou on WhatsApp with your name, matric number, and course code to receive them directly.

Can I invite Dr. Tambou to speak or minister?

Absolutely — revivals, conferences, youth programmes, and deliverance meetings. Send your church/ministry name, proposed dates, and theme via WhatsApp (+234 816 576 6998) or email, and allow at least 2–3 weeks for scheduling.

Do you supervise research and dissertations?

Yes, from Diploma projects to Master's theses across Sciences and Arts: topic framing, literature review, methodology, APA 7th editing, and journal formatting. Share your level, department, and deadline when you reach out.

Do you coach football teams and athletes?

Yes. Licensed by the National Institute for Sports, Surulere, Lagos. Training covers tactics, technique, fitness, teamwork, plus sports psychology — focus, confidence, and composure. Teams and individuals are welcome.

How fast will I get a response?

WhatsApp is fastest (usually within 24–48 hours, Mon–Fri). For urgent pastoral matters, call directly. Email works best for documents and longer academic enquiries.
